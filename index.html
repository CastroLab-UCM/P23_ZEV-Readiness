ZEV Readiness Framework Dashboard

This dashboard helps communities evaluate their readiness for Zero-Emission Vehicle (ZEV) adoption across six key categories: infrastructure, mobility access, supportive policies, outreach, workforce, and equity.

Score each indicator, view overall readiness results, and identify areas that need improvement. The framework supports planning, discussion, and decision-making by highlighting strengths, gaps, and opportunities. Scroll to the 📋 Scoring Table or 🔧 What to Work on Next for priority-based improvement recommendations.

How to Use This Dashboard
1
Browse the Indicator Cards
Each card represents one measurable aspect of ZEV readiness — such as charging infrastructure, workforce training, or equity access. Read the short description to understand what that indicator is measuring in your community.
2
Check the Suggested Unit & Range
Each card shows a Suggested Unit (how the indicator is measured, e.g. "% uptime") and a Suggested Range (benchmark values from research). Use these to understand where your community currently stands before assigning a score.
3
Assign a Score — Two Easy Ways
Option A — Quick buttons: Click Not Ready (1–3), Somewhat Ready (>3–7), or Ready (>7–10) based on your community's current situation. The score updates automatically.

Option B — Slider: Drag the slider to any number from 1 to 10 for a more precise score. Use "Show readiness conditions" on each card to read exactly what qualifies as Not Ready, Somewhat Ready, or Ready — with real data thresholds to compare against.
4
Read the Readiness Summary
The Overall Readiness Summary panel updates live as you score. It shows your average score out of 10 and how many indicators fall into each readiness level. The Category Scores bars reveal which of the six pillars are strongest and which need the most attention.
5
Review What to Work on Next
Scroll down to the "What to Work on Next" section. For every indicator it gives you a plain-language action step based on your score — whether you need to start from scratch, build on what you have, or maintain strong progress. This is your practical to-do list.
6
Export or Print Your Results
Use the toolbar at the top to save your work:
📥 Export CSV — downloads a spreadsheet you can open in Excel or Google Sheets, with all scores in one table.
🖨 Print / Save PDF — opens your browser's print dialog. Select "Save as PDF" to create a shareable report.
💡 Tip — Use the Search & Filter bar
Use the bar at the top of the page to search by keyword (e.g. "charging" or "equity"), or filter by category or measurement type to focus on a specific set of indicators. The summary scores always reflect only the currently visible indicators.

Indicator Scoring Cards

Each indicator includes a short description, suggested unit, suggested working range, and a manual readiness score from 1 to 10. Scores are adjusted with a slider so users can move along the line instead of selecting from a dropdown. Community members can also choose a readiness option directly from the criteria file for each indicator. Click “Show readiness conditions” inside any indicator card to see the Not Ready, Somewhat Ready, and Ready conditions before selecting one.
  • Not Ready: Use this when the file indicates no relevant actions, no systems in place, no documented planning, or no supporting evidence.
  • Somewhat Ready: Use this when some actions are underway, monitoring is partial, coverage is incomplete, or planning exists but is not yet comprehensive.
  • Ready: Use this when the indicator meets the strongest condition in the file, with consistent implementation, complete coverage, public documentation, or integration into plans.
Use the notes field for workshop comments, assumptions, or next actions.
